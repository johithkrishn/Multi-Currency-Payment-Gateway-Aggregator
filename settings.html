FX rates & demo settings

Every value here is a demonstration assumption. Changing them never rewrites saved transactions — each record keeps its own snapshot.

Exchange-rate source

Live fetches real reference rates — European Central Bank via Frankfurter, with ExchangeRate-API and currency-api as fallbacks — and re-checks every few minutes. Reference rates are published once per business day, are not executable quotes, and no real payment is ever made. Demo uses the editable illustrative table below. If every live source is unreachable, checkout falls back to the demo table and says so.
